About The Best DIY Home

The Best DIY Home is a home decor blog and DIY project website about one simple idea: a beautiful home does not have to be an expensive home.

Our Mission

Our purpose is to help people make their homes beautiful, comfortable, stylish and creative without spending too much money. We do that by sharing practical DIY home decoration ideas, affordable room transformations, handmade decorations, budget-friendly makeover guidance and renter-friendly solutions.

Who This Site Is For

This site is for beginners and home decor enthusiasts: people who love DIY decoration, homeowners looking for affordable improvements, renters who want to decorate without permanent changes, and anyone looking for creative ideas for bedrooms, living rooms, walls, bathrooms and seasonal decorating.

What We Cover

Our eight categories are Bedroom Makeover, DIY Wall Decor, Dollar Store DIY Decor, Renter-Friendly Decor, Budget Living Room Makeover, Handmade Home Decorations, Seasonal Home Decor and Small Bathroom Makeover. Keeping the same categories everywhere helps you find related ideas quickly.

How We Work

We aim to explain projects clearly and honestly, in plain language, with realistic materials and steps. We do not claim to have personally tested or photographed a project unless that is actually true, and we clearly disclose affiliate relationships when they apply — see our Affiliate Disclosure.
