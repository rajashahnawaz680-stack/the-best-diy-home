Why DIY With a Budget

A budget is not a limitation here — it is the method. It keeps projects honest, finishable and kind to the home you already live in.

Real Budgets

A small budget forces better choices: fix what you notice every day first, reuse what you already own, and spend only on the gaps that remain.

Renter-Safe Options

Many of our ideas need no drilling and no permanent changes — textiles, lighting, layout and removable touches do most of the work.

Weekend Doable

Projects are broken into small, finishable steps you can complete in a weekend or two, then live with before deciding what to do next.
