DIY Safety

DIY projects can involve sharp tools, power tools, ladders, paints, adhesives and heavy items. Always read and follow the instructions and safety warnings provided by the manufacturers of your tools and materials, use appropriate protective equipment, and do not attempt a project you are not confident you can do safely.

Renters

Renter-friendly ideas are general suggestions only. Tenancy agreements differ, so check your own agreement and, where needed, get your landlord’s permission before making changes — including using removable products.
