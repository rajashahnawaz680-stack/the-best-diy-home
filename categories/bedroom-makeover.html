Bedroom Makeover

Your bedroom should feel calm and personal, even when the budget is small. This section gathers practical ways to refresh the room you start and end each day in, from simple decoration changes to affordable furniture updates. You will find ideas that work in both spacious rooms and compact spaces, with an emphasis on comfort, storage and a pulled-together look. Start with one corner, one wall or one piece of furniture and build from there.

What You’ll Find Here & Getting Started

Practical advice for beginners starting a bedroom makeover project:

  • Start by decluttering and rearranging what you already own before buying anything new.
  • Choose a simple colour palette of two or three colours and repeat it in textiles and decor.
  • Improve lighting first — a warm lamp often changes a room more than new furniture.
  • Measure your space and any furniture before starting a makeover project.
