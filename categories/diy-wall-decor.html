DIY Wall Decor

Blank walls are one of the easiest opportunities in any home. This section is full of handmade and budget-friendly ways to add colour, texture and personality to your walls, whether you prefer framed art, shelves or paper projects. Most ideas here use simple tools and materials you can find easily. Pick a single wall to begin with and treat it as a small, finishable project.

What You’ll Find Here & Getting Started

Practical advice for beginners starting a diy wall decor project:

  • Plan the arrangement on the floor or with paper templates before making any holes.
  • Keep a consistent frame colour or material to make a mixed gallery wall feel intentional.
  • Check what your wall is made of and use fixings suited to it.
  • Step back and view the wall from the doorway before finalising positions.
