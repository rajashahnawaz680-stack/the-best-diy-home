Budget Living Room Makeover

The living room works hard: it is where you relax, host and spend everyday time. This section shows how to refresh it without replacing everything, using layout changes, furniture makeovers and simple decorative projects. Ideas here suit small rooms as well as larger spaces, and most can be done in stages as time and budget allow. Begin with how the room is used, then decorate around that.

What You’ll Find Here & Getting Started

Practical advice for beginners starting a budget living room makeover project:

  • Rearrange the layout first — a new arrangement can make a room feel new for free.
  • Give the sofa and main seating a focal point, such as a rug, artwork or a styled table.
  • Use trays and baskets to group small items so surfaces look tidy, not cluttered.
  • Refresh soft furnishings — cushions and throws are an affordable way to change colour.
