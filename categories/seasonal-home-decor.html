Seasonal Home Decor

Small seasonal changes keep a home feeling fresh without a full redecoration. For now, this category focuses on the Christmas and New Year season — wreaths, garlands, tree styling, fairy lights and festive table decor that make the holidays feel special without a big spend. Reuse a core set of neutral pieces and swap the accents around them; everything stores away easily when the season ends.

What You’ll Find Here & Getting Started

Practical advice for beginners starting a seasonal home decor project:

  • Keep a labelled storage box per season so decorations are easy to find and reuse.
  • Build around neutral basics and add seasonal colour with small, storable items.
  • Focus seasonal effort on the entryway, living room and dining table for the biggest effect.
  • Pack fragile items carefully and check lights and batteries before storing them.
