Renter-Friendly Decor

Renting does not have to mean living with a space that does not feel like yours. This section focuses on reversible decorating: changes you can enjoy now and remove when you move, without drilling, painting permanent colours or damaging walls. You will find storage, wallpaper and styling ideas suited to apartments and shared homes. Always check your tenancy agreement before starting, as rules vary.

What You’ll Find Here & Getting Started

Practical advice for beginners starting a renter-friendly decor project:

  • Check your lease and ask your landlord before using adhesives or removable products.
  • Test removable products in a hidden spot and follow removal instructions carefully.
  • Use furniture, textiles and lighting to change a room when walls cannot be changed.
  • Keep original fittings you swap out, labelled, so you can restore them later.
