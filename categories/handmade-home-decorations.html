Handmade Home Decorations

Handmade pieces add something ready-made decor cannot: a personal touch and a story. This section is for makers of all levels, with craft ideas for vases, centrepieces, table decorations and small accessories for around the home. Several projects reuse or recycle materials you may already have, which helps keep costs down. Choose a project that matches your time and tools, and enjoy the process as much as the result.

What You’ll Find Here & Getting Started

Practical advice for beginners starting a handmade home decorations project:

  • Read the whole project through and gather materials before you start.
  • Practise a new technique on scrap material first.
  • Let paint, glue and finishes dry fully between steps for a cleaner result.
  • Work in a ventilated area and follow safety guidance for tools and materials.
